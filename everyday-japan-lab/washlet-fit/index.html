Formula, assumptions and input limits

What is compared?

The worksheet records dimensions and converts inches to millimetres using 1 in = 25.4 mm. It flags unknown or mismatched voltage classes and unresolved outlet and water connections. It does not contain a product database or universal dimensional pass threshold.

Physical bounds reject empty, non-finite or implausibly large entries; accepting a number is not evidence of a real fit. Exact voltage and frequency, certification, mounting range, rear contour, hose access and local backflow requirements still need verification.
