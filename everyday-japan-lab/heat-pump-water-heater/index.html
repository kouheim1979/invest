Formula, assumptions and input limits

Annual heat and electricity

Useful draw heat = litres/day × 4.186 × (delivered °C − inlet °C) ÷ 3,600 × days
Modelled heat = useful draw heat × (1 + loss % / 100)
Electricity = modelled heat × [(1 − backup fraction) / COP + backup fraction]

Water density is approximated as 1 kg/L; heat capacity is 4.186 kJ/(kg·°C). Backup heat is modelled at COP 1. The percentage-loss model scales with draw volume and does not represent fixed standing losses at zero draw. Days must be an integer from 1–366; COP 1–8 and the temperature bounds are model input limits, not equipment operating limits.

Baseline and payback

Baseline energy = modelled heat / baseline efficiency fraction
Annual saving = baseline energy × baseline price − electricity × electricity price
Simple payback = additional upfront cost / positive annual saving

No result is a quote or sizing recommendation. Fixed tariffs, finance, maintenance, space-conditioning effects, recovery capacity, hourly climate and price changes are excluded. Use the same thermal boundary for both systems. COP 2/3/4 rows are illustrative sensitivities. Defaults are examples only.
