Emergency water: compare the Japanese and US planning allowances

Different allowances cover different needs. Pick a local starting point before multiplying litres.

By Everyday Japan Lab · Reviewed 2026-09-24 · Research and planning, not product certification

Two references, two contexts

ReferencePlanning amountWhat to remember
Japan, Cabinet OfficeAbout 3 L per person per day for drinking waterDo not assume this covers all washing, toilet or other household water.
United States, CDCAt least 1 US gallon per person per day; at least 3 days, with 2 weeks if possibleRead the current guidance for its included uses and circumstances requiring more.

One US liquid gallon is 3.785411784 litres. We use that conversion in the calculator; the extra digits improve unit conversion, not certainty about a person’s needs. The two source allowances are not interchangeable claims of sufficient water for every household. [1, 2]

Count containers after subtracting usable stock

For an illustrative four-person, seven-day plan at 3 L per person per day, the target is 84 L. With 20 L already available, the gap is 64 L. At 2 L per bottle that is 32 additional bottles. If the gap is not a whole number of containers, round up.

Think about how the containers will be moved and accessed. Storing the entire reserve behind heavy furniture may make counting easy while making use difficult. Keep evacuation routes clear and ask for local advice if storage weight or structural loading is a concern.

Storage quality is a separate check

Use the current public-health guidance for suitable containers, labelling, storage and replacement. Follow the dates and instructions for commercially bottled water. A quantity calculator cannot decide whether water remains safe. We do not provide improvised disinfection recipes or treat appearance as a safety test.

Plan additional needs explicitly, including pets and any local conditions the guideline identifies. Enter a custom rate when appropriate, and write down the source or reason so that the next household review can understand the number.
