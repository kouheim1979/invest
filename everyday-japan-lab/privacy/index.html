Privacy — development preview

This notice describes the current GitHub Pages preview, not a future advertising site.

By Everyday Japan Lab · Reviewed 2026-09-24 · Research and planning, not product certification

Who publishes this preview?

Everyday Japan Lab is published by Kouhei. The contact page provides a public issue channel for editorial corrections and technical problems.

Calculator inputs

The five calculators run in your browser. This site’s code does not send their inputs to a server, store them in cookies or local storage, or create user accounts. Reloading the page discards the calculation. A printed or saved copy is under your control.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages serves this preview and may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Our lack of analytics tags does not mean the host receives no connection information. Following a source link or opening GitHub’s issue form takes you to another provider with its own practices.

Advertising and measurement

There are no AdSense ads, affiliate tracking links, GA4 tags, embedded videos or third-party web fonts in this preview. We do not request optional advertising or analytics consent for services we have not enabled.

Feedback

GitHub issues are public. Do not post private contact details, health information, account credentials or sensitive household information. A private contact route must be configured before inviting private enquiries.

Future production site

A Blogger production site will use a separate notice describing its actual platform and enabled services. Advertising or analytics will require an updated disclosure and appropriate consent arrangements before activation. This preview notice is not that production notice.
