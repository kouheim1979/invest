Before you use the result

CHAdeMO’s bidirectional technology reference does not prove that your car supports home backup. Never infer safe household connection or life-critical runtime from this calculation.

Formula, assumptions and input limits

Energy budget

Available AC energy (kWh) = usable capacity × (starting % − reserve %) / 100 × efficiency % / 100
Runtime (hours) = available AC energy / [(average load W + standby W) / 1,000]

Equal starting and reserve charge gives zero usable energy. A greater reserve is rejected. Real loads cycle and equipment has surge and continuous-power limits. Capacity can change with age and temperature; state-of-charge reporting and usable-capacity boundaries may differ. No connector, vehicle, charger, inverter, transfer switch, islanding, solar charging or local grid approval is checked.
