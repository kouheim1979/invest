Before you use the result

A planning compartment count is not a procurement specification. Compare longer collection delays and peak periods; check every relevant parcel-size class.

Formula, assumptions and input limits

Steady-state planning model

Mean daily arrivals = homes × parcels/home/week / 7
Mean occupied compartments = daily arrivals × mean collection hours / 24
Planning compartments = round up(mean occupied × peak multiplier / occupancy fraction)

This applies an average-flow relationship with additional chosen headroom. It assumes one parcel per compartment and steady flows. Zero arrivals or zero dwell time gives zero modelled occupancy; it does not prove that an actual installation needs no boxes. Inputs must be finite, with whole homes and positive occupancy. Target occupancy means the share of adjusted capacity planned to be filled; it is not a probability of finding a free box.

The model omits parcel size mix, multiple carriers, clustered arrivals, holidays, rejected parcels, maintenance, accessibility, fire routes and lock workflow. Collect hourly arrival and retrieval data, then ask a vendor for a design and service-level analysis. MLIT’s redelivery programme establishes the policy context, not the multipliers in this tool.
