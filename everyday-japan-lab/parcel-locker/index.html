Parcel-locker capacity estimator

A simple occupancy model for apartment and multifamily planning. It turns delivery rate and average pickup delay into a rough number of compartments.

—

Planning estimate only: expected concurrent parcels ≈ homes × parcels/week ÷ 7 × holding-hours ÷ 24 × peak factor. Real sizing also needs parcel-size mix, failed pickups, seasonal peaks, carrier workflow, accessibility and fire/building rules.

Japan's transport ministry actively promotes parcel lockers and unattended delivery as part of reducing redelivery. MLIT source.
