Formula, assumptions and input limits

Calculation

Target = people × days × daily allowance
Additional supply = max(0, target − usable stock)
Water containers to add = round up(additional litres / container litres)

All people, days, meals and toilet-use counts are whole numbers. Fractional litres are allowed. Quantity limits prevent nonsensical input; they are not recommended preparedness limits. The Japan reference is about 3 L drinking water per person per day. The US reference is 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L and has a different scope. Changing the water allowance selects Custom.

The tool does not assess water quality, nutrition, food-storage safety, allergies, pet needs, medical supplies, local sewer conditions or disposal rules. Toilet uses are not automatically equal to packages or bags. Confirm product yield and local guidance. Print a copy to keep your plan; nothing is saved.

Sources and scope

Primary references checked 2026-09-24. Manufacturer statements are identified as such.

  1. Cabinet Office Japan — Public preparedness guidance
    Three days minimum and preferably a week; roughly 3 L drinking water per person per day. Guidance is not observed adoption.
  2. MAFF Japan — Household food-stock guidance
    Rotate familiar foods; recommendation does not establish household compliance.
  3. CDC — Public emergency-water guidance
    At least one US gallon per person per day for three days; aim for two weeks if possible. Scope differs from Japan's drinking-water figure.
  4. METI Japan — Disaster toilet working-group material
    Five uses per person per day is a planning assumption; individual and product needs vary.
