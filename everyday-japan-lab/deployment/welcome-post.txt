<p class="eyebrow">Practical systems from everyday Japan.</p>
<p class="lede">Will a bidet seat fit your toilet? Could a heat-pump water heater suit your home? How much emergency water should you store? Start with a practical question, then check the conditions that matter where you live.</p>
<p>Everyday Japan Lab investigates systems used in everyday Japanese life and explains where they may help elsewhere. Local climate, voltage, building rules, available products and installation costs can change the answer.</p>
