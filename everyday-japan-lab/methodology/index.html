How we choose topics

We do not publish a topic just because it appears distinctly Japanese. We look for a documented system or product with established use in Japan and a real problem that people elsewhere are trying to solve.

SignalWhat we look for
Japan maturityA system, product or habit already used at meaningful scale in Japan.
Global timingEvidence of overseas growth, policy attention, infrastructure change or rising product availability.
Commercial intentPeople need to compare, size, retrofit, buy or install something — not just read trivia.
Tool potentialA calculator, checklist, compatibility profile or estimator can improve the decision.
TrustClaims can be grounded in official or first-party documentation.

Current launch set

Warm-water bidet seats, CO₂ heat-pump water heating, household disaster stockpiling, EV-to-home backup and parcel lockers.

We intentionally avoid giving professional engineering, medical or legal advice where a simple web tool cannot safely replace a qualified assessment.
