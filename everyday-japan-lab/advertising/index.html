Advertising and affiliate policy

Editorial decisions are based on usefulness and evidence, not payment.

By Everyday Japan Lab · Reviewed 2026-09-24 · Research and planning, not product certification

Current status

This development preview carries no advertising, affiliate links or sponsored content. Ordinary manufacturer and public-source links are references, not paid recommendations.

If monetization is introduced

  • Advertisements will be labelled and visually separated from navigation, calculator controls and results.
  • Affiliate relationships will be disclosed near the relevant links, with any programme-required site disclosure.
  • Sponsored articles will be labelled at the beginning and identify the sponsor.
  • Payment will not buy a recommendation rank or remove a material limitation.
  • We will not describe an untested product as tested or the best.
  • Manufacturer claims will remain attributed, with their test conditions where relevant.

Possible revenue sources

Contextually relevant ads, approved retail affiliate programmes, qualified local service referrals and clearly labelled sponsorship may be considered. Programme availability and approval are not assumed. No revenue level is promised.

Reader experience

Tools must remain usable without confusing clicks or exaggerated urgency. Privacy information and any required consent choice must reflect the services actually enabled. Commercial suitability does not override safety or source quality.
