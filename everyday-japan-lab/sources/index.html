Primary source register

What each reference can establish — and what it cannot.

By Everyday Japan Lab · Reviewed 2026-09-24 · Research and planning, not product certification

This register covers the published guides and tools. The broader candidate inventory has its own source table. All references were checked on 2026-09-24; linked providers may change their documents.

Sources and scope

Primary references checked 2026-09-24. Manufacturer statements are identified as such.

  1. TOTO — Manufacturer history / sales report
    Long-running WASHLET business and reported overseas sales; not independent market penetration.
  2. TOTO USA — Model-specific technical drawing
    Dimensions vary by model, bowl and tank contour; no universal fit threshold.
  3. Brondell — Manufacturer fit guide
    Overseas fit guidance exists; does not establish search volume.
  4. Brondell — Product manual
    Electrical and plumbing rules for the named model, not all bidets.
  5. Japan Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Industry Association — Industry shipment history
    EcoCute cumulative shipments exceeded ten million in March 2025; shipments are not installed households.
  6. IEA — International market analysis
    Mature Japanese heat-pump market and CO2 water heating; regional growth differs.
  7. US Department of Energy — Technical explanation
    Heat transfer principle, not a guaranteed seasonal COP.
  8. ENERGY STAR — Government programme technical guidance
    US integrated HPWH design constraints; do not transfer these unchanged to split CO2 systems.
  9. ENERGY STAR — Government programme installation guidance
    Airflow, installation and operating conditions matter.
  10. Cabinet Office Japan — Public preparedness guidance
    Three days minimum and preferably a week; roughly 3 L drinking water per person per day. Guidance is not observed adoption.
  11. MAFF Japan — Household food-stock guidance
    Rotate familiar foods; recommendation does not establish household compliance.
  12. CDC — Public emergency-water guidance
    At least one US gallon per person per day for three days; aim for two weeks if possible. Scope differs from Japan's drinking-water figure.
  13. METI Japan — Disaster toilet working-group material
    Five uses per person per day is a planning assumption; individual and product needs vary.
  14. CHAdeMO Association — Industry technical overview
    Bidirectional technology exists; vehicle, charger, grid and islanding approval must all match.
  15. MLIT Japan — Public logistics policy
    Lockers and delivery practices promoted; no measured saving is assumed in this inventory.
  16. Panasonic — Manufacturer product catalogue
    Apartment parcel lockers exist; service capacity requires site data.
