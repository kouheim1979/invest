About Japan Ahead

Japan Ahead looks for ordinary Japanese solutions that may be useful elsewhere: home equipment, energy systems, delivery infrastructure, disaster readiness and compact-living ideas.

The goal is not to claim that Japan invented everything or that Japanese solutions are automatically better. Each topic is treated as a question: what problem does it solve, why did it become normal in Japan, and what would someone overseas need to check before adopting it?

Whenever a topic can be made more useful with a calculator, checklist or sizing worksheet, we build one. Product fit, local building rules, electrical standards and installation requirements still need to be checked against local regulations and manufacturer documentation.

Editorial principle

We prefer primary and official sources: government agencies, standards bodies, international agencies and manufacturers describing their own products. We distinguish a documented fact from our own practical calculation or editorial judgment.
