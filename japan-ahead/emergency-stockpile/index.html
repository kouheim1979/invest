Japanese-style emergency stockpile calculator

Japan's Cabinet Office recommends at least three days of household supplies, ideally a week, and suggests “rolling stock”: buy a little extra of everyday food and replace what you use.

—

Water uses the Japanese government guideline of 3 litres per person per day. Meals are shown as a planning count of three meals per person per day, not a nutrition prescription. Add medications, infant/pet needs, sanitation, lighting, power and local hazards separately.

Source: Cabinet Office, Government of Japan.
